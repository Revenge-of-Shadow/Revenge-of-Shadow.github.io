Finest imperfection


I am fond of her inconspicuousness.

20260407 1105


I have not drawn specifically portraits, as well as I have not drawn on paper anything of serious scale, for a long time. And there is that she looks not quite as she should but still nice. Her face is not that of Gumi I perceive. But this is what is nice.
The imperfection of having made a nice drawing that veered into my style is very fine. If she looked exactly as I wanted her, it might have been boring, although completely not undesirable. What she is now, however, has a spirit of its own.

The Gumi I love lives in “Just for Tonight”. Only in this song the melody, her voice and visual appearance, devised by two different artists, create the beauty like none else.
