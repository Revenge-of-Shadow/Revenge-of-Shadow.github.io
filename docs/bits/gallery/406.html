In a shopping mall I noticed a girl with wonderful scarlet hair and a lovely face. This much I could tell from about twenty meters away. I passed by more than once, but I kept coming back, so I decided to draw her.
I did. Just as she was leaving, I slid her the sketch and started walking away swiftly as I noticed her excitement.
She caught up with me several seconds later and thanked me. I smiled and dug myself deeper into my scarf with my ears being noticeably hot. She was flustered as she talked to her friends in Russian language. So I complimented her hair and strode off.
